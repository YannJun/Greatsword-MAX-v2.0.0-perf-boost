Greatsword MAX v2.0 move list:

Arrange the 6 skills as below:
☐: Windstorm Slash (烈风斩)
△: Diluvian Strike (贯江)
〇: Tidal Wrath (退潮)
✕: Inspirit (斗志昂扬)
☐+✕: Arc of Might (退魔剑圣斩)
△+〇: Vow of Blood (血染契约) this one is optional, Warfarer can change it to weapon switch

Below 3 features can be turned on/off in game UI menu GreatswordMAX_Warrior (default on)
-Warrior special debuff: when the Warrior takes damage, Stamina is reduced according to the HP loss. When Stamina being deducted to 0, the debuff will stop for 10 seconds.
-Chain of Blows boost: when performing Light Attack or Windstorm Slash, every success just timing chain +10% damage and recovers 20 Stamina, maximum +60% damage, the damage buff last 1.8 sec
-Attack Buff: When the 1st hit of Arc of Might lands on enemy, the 2nd hit will have 20% damage increase. Same mechanism applies to Razing Sweep

Hold ☐ to enter charging state:
△: Knoll Breaker (press R2, ✕ or hold △ to perform different follow up moves, double press △ to perform ComboPoke)
✕: Skyward Sunder (press △, R2, or hold ✕ to perform different follow up moves, double press ✕ to perform Skyward Sunder Combo)
R2: Rending Sweep (press △, R2, or ✕ to perform different follow up moves)
L3: Catapult Launch

Hold ☐ to fully Charged (Level 1 charge):
△: Mountain Breaker (press △ while in the air to perform Diluvian Strike, press ☐, R2, or ✕ to perform different follow up moves)
✕: Heavenward Sunder (press △ to perform a powerful follow up attack in the air, hold △ will perform the ending AirSlamCombo. press ☐, △, R2, or long press ✕ to perform different follow up moves. Double press ✕ right after landing will perform SlashUpCombo)
R2: Razing Sweep (press ☐ or △ to perform different follow up moves)
L1: Indomitable Lash (Level 2 charge)
L3: Catapult Launch

Basic skill extensions:
L1+(☐+✕): SuperRoar, able to hit down small flying enemies like Harpy (not DLC flying enemies at the moment)
SuperRoarBuff: Long press ✕ when performing SuperRoar, activates buff mode, +30% damage, last 9 seconds, cost extra 120 Stamina. During the Buff, Player will not loss balance when standing on big monsters, will not be bounced off when hitting hard surface. And you are immune to the Stamina debuff during this 9 seconds
Chain of Blows boost: when performing Light Attack or Windstorm Slash, every success just timing chain +10% damage and recovers 20 Stamina, maximum +60% damage, the damage buff last 1.8 sec
L1+△: Advanced Ravening Lunge (press ☐, △, ✕, R2 or L1+☐, L1+△ to perform different follow up moves. Press L1+✕ to perform SuperFarJump. Press L3 to perform ThrustSpeedUp, speed up for 0.6 sec, cost 80 stamina)
L1+△ (in the air): Diluvian Strike (with higher damage according to the height)
L1+〇: Tidal Wrath (when successful counter, press ☐, △, R2, or ✕ to perform different counter attacks)
L1+✕: Inspirit with 6 seconds self healing
L1+✕ (long press ✕): SuperJump
L1+✕ (in the air): Air Heavenward, able to hit down large flying enemies like Griffin)
L1+☐: DashSlashUp (press △ while in the air to perform Diluvian Strike, press ☐, R2, or ✕ to perform different follow up moves)

Tackle extensions:
R1: Tackle (it can cancel all ground moves except Arc of Might). Press △, R2, or ✕ to perform different follow up moves. Press R1 or L1 to perform BareHandAttack, hit unbalanced or lie down enemy will enter Finishing animation, press △, R2, or ✕ to perform different follow up moves.
R1 (while Thrust running or after rolling): a stronger tackle with higher damage and stun effect, cost 80 stamina
Long press R1 (while Level 2 or Level 3 charging): Tackle and return to the charging state

Special moves:
Indomitable Lash: when fully charged, press ☐ to enter Level 3 charge, press △, R2, or ✕ to perform different follow up moves. After performing a perfect Indomitable Lash, press ☐ to enter Level 3 charge, and then you need to hold L1 again to keep charging for Arc of Might
Arc of Might: When fully charged, just release to perform One-Hit Arc of Might, press ☐ with perfect timing to perform Ultimate Arc of Might. Press △, R2, or ✕ to perform different moves as Charged actions)
Uncharged Arc of Might: Press △ or ✕ to perform different follow up moves
Roll: Double press 〇 (it can cancel all ground moves except Arc of Might. Press ☐, △, R2, or ✕ to perform different follow up moves)
SuperHeavyAttack: Double press △, faster and higher damage HeavyAttack but cost 120 Stamina when hit
FlamingSowrd: Long press ✕ when performing UpperCut series of acts will activate Fire Enchantment, last 12 sec, cost 60 stamina
HighAirSlam: When performing HeavyAirAttack at higher air, hold △ will perform ending AirSlamCombo, cost 120 stamina
RisingWhirlSlam: special life saving skill RisingWhirlSlam (press R2), it will rise you damage down position and hit down enemies around you, but it has 60 sec cool down, see the golden light show up again means the cool down is over
